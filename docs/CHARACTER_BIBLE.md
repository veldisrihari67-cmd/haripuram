# CHARACTER BIBLE — HARIPURAM (హరిపురం)

This bible covers all 75 recurring characters in `characters.json`, using the same ids. Language layers: **native** (దేశ్య), **traditional**, **home**, **balanced**, **modern-spoken**, **literary** (సాహిత్య), **sanskrit** (సంస్కృత ప్రభావిత).

Teaching principle: the learner (Srihari, "Pandu") meets each register through a person, never through a list. Each character owns a slice of the language.

---

## PART 1 — PANDU HOUSE (పండు ఇల్లు)

### 1. Srihari — శ్రీహరి ("Pandu" / పండు) · `srihari`
- **Age / role:** 18, the learner's avatar; intermediate second-year student
- **Biography:** Raised partly outside the village, he speaks Telugu with an English mix. He returns to Haripuram for his final school years and treats the village as the thing he must read.
- **Education:** Intermediate second year at the mandal college
- **Occupation:** Student (విద్యార్థి)
- **Speech style:** Modern Telugu learner, English-mixed
- **Language layer:** modern-spoken, moving towards native then Sanskrit Telugu
- **Relationships:** Grandson of Narayana and Mahalakshamma; son of Lakshmaiah and Anasuya; brother of Divya, Anjali, Kiran; nephew to most of the village
- **Story purpose:** The viewpoint through whom all stories are experienced
- **Educational purpose:** Models the learner's questions; his errors are corrected kindly by elders
- **Vocabulary strengths:** Questions (ఏమిటి, ఎందుకు, ఎక్కడ), place names, English-Telugu mixed words
- **Favourite places:** Library, Banyan Tree, Cheruvu
- **Daily routine:** Sweeps the courtyard with his grandfather, takes the college bus, reads in the library, walks the cheruvu bund, asks his mother for the day's new words

### 2. Lakshmaiah — లక్ష్మయ్య ("Latchanna") · `lakshmaiah`
- **Age / role:** 48, father; agricultural officer
- **Biography:** Son of a farmer who studied agriculture and returned to serve the mandal. He holds the house together with patience rather than speeches.
- **Education:** B.Sc. Agriculture
- **Occupation:** Agricultural officer (వ్యవసాయాధికారి)
- **Speech style:** Balanced, official words in the field but simple at home
- **Language layer:** balanced
- **Relationships:** Husband of Anasuya; son of Narayana and Mahalakshamma; brother of Venkata Rao, Ramesh, Sujatha; father-in-law of Karthik
- **Story purpose:** Bridge between scientific agriculture and inherited farming
- **Educational purpose:** Teaches agricultural and government vocabulary through concrete field examples
- **Vocabulary strengths:** Soil, water, irrigation, seed, scheme, subsidy
- **Favourite places:** Fields, Pandu House, Banyan Tree
- **Daily routine:** Motorcycle rounds at dawn, mandal office files, evening stop on the cheruvu bund, explains one farm word to Pandu at night
